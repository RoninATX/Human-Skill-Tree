Add the human dimension — mentors, peer validation, community-driven mastery verification, and shared skill trees.

## Key Questions
- How do users validate each other's skills? (endorsements, reviews, challenges?)
- What role do mentors/coaches play in the system?
- Can skill trees be shared or compared between users?
- How do we handle trust/reputation in peer validation?
- Do we support groups/communities around specific domains?
